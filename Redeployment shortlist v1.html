Redeployment shortlist wireframe, 40 keepers, synthetic data

Skills come from what each keeper wrote in the logbooks. Certificates come from the training record, because a log entry cannot prove one.

Reading this page. Skill strength by log entries: ■■■ strong (8 or more), ■■□ some (3 to 7), ■□□ trace (1 to 2), □□□ none. Match rate = requirements met, mandatory ones counting double. Strong skill or valid certificate = full credit, some = half, trace = a sliver, lapsed or incomplete certificate = 30%. Things the records cannot show (medical, passport) are listed but not scored. Stood-by and read-only HV lines are not counted as switching. Date of birth and payroll numbers are not loaded.
